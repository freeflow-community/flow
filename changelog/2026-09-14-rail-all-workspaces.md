# Workspace rail lists Slack teams and other servers (web)

- [web] The left workspace rail shows every connection's workspaces, as the menu and chooser do since #592; a Slack team carries a small Slack mark, and clicking another connection's workspace switches to it.

## Feature

- **Your Slack teams are on the workspace rail.** On the web, the icons down the left edge now include your Slack teams (with a small Slack mark) and workspaces from your other Flow servers, one click away.
