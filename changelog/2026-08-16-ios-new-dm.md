# iOS: start a new DM

- `[ios]` A "+" on the sidebar's Direct Messages header opens a people picker
  (searchable, multi-select up to 8 for a group DM) and lands in the new
  conversation. The header now renders even with no DMs — it was the only way
  in, hidden exactly when you needed it.
- `[ios]` A **Message** button on a member's profile card opens the DM with
  them. Picking someone you already have a DM with opens it: the server route
  dedupes by member set, for group DMs too.
- `[qa]` `NewDmTests` covers both entry points, the duplicate case and a group
  DM; `FLOW_DEBUG_OPEN_NEW_DM` / `FLOW_DEBUG_NEW_DM` drive it headlessly.

## Feature

- **Start a direct message on iPhone.** Tap "+" next to Direct Messages, pick
  one person — or several for a group chat — and you're in. There's also a
  Message button on anyone's profile card. Until now the iPhone app could only
  open a conversation someone else had started.
