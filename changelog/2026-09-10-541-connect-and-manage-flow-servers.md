# Connect and manage Flow servers

- [web] [macos] [ios] Add servers and invites, sign in on the destination, and select workspaces grouped by hostname and account.
- [web] [macos] [ios] Scope navigation, drafts, media and management actions to their owning connection; clear private data on local sign-out.
- [ios] Route sharing and notifications through the selected registry connection.

## Feature

- **Keep multiple Flow servers together.** Connect another server, add its workspaces, and switch between accounts without signing out of your other servers.
