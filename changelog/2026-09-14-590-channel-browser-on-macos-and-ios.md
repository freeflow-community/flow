# Channel browser on macOS and iOS (#590)

- `[macos]` `[ios]` "Browse all" at the end of Channels (sidebar / ≡ drawer) opens the channel browser: search by name or topic, member counts, Join / ✓ Joined, include-archived toggle. Replaces the inline Browse list.
- `[macos]` `[ios]` Archived channels open read-only from the browser: banner, full history, no composer (channel or thread), no react/pin/edit/delete, invite or channel options. They stay out of the local cache, so never reach the sidebar or unreads.
- Closes the #588 Parity gap.

## Feature

- **Browse every channel on Mac and iPhone too.** "Browse all" at the end of your Channels list opens a searchable list of every public channel with a Join button, and "Include archived" lets you read old channels' history.
