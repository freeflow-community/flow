# Workspace avatars stay on the rail when another connection is open (web)

- [web] Workspace bindings record each workspace's avatar and follow the connection's live list (renames, joins, leaves; hidden stays hidden), and the rail loads another connection's avatar through that connection.

## Feature

- **Workspace pictures stay put.** On the web, your workspace avatars no longer turn into letters on the left rail while a Slack team or another server is open.
