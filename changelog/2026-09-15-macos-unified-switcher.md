# macOS: one workspace list across connections

- [macos] The rail, the sidebar workspace menu and the workspace chooser list every connection's workspaces — Slack teams and other Flow servers included — with their avatars and unread badges; picking one switches the window to that connection.
- [macos] Registry bindings carry the workspace avatar, refreshed after each workspace load, so a background connection draws with current names and marks.
- [qa] `WorkspaceSwitcherEntryTests` (9 cases).

## Feature

- **All your workspaces in one rail on the Mac.** Slack teams and workspaces from your other Flow servers now sit alongside this server's, and clicking one switches to it.
