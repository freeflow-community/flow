# Slack provider step 4: reconciliation, honest notifications, native parity

- [server] Connector sends are idempotent per `client_msg_id`; a timed-out send is an unknown outcome reconciled against Slack by author, text and `ts` before any retry, never posted twice. Malformed events are acknowledged and dropped without breaking the stream.
- [server] A configured `flow://` client origin is a native client: the OAuth start/poll routes accept it without an `Origin` header and the callback returns to `flow://slack/connected` with only the operation id.
- [web] Events insert in `ts` order, a stream gap refetches only the visible channel's first page, and a truncated transcript says so; malformed events are counted and dropped.
- [web] [macos] [ios] `notifications` reads **limited**: mentions and DMs alert only while Flow is open. No client registers its push token with the connector; the push design that would close this is in `docs/dev/SLACK_CONNECTOR.md`, not built.
- [macos] [ios] Connect Slack from the switcher (`ASWebAuthenticationSession` + PKCE against the connector), a `SlackBackend` per Slack connection, and the sync engine routed through `WorkspaceBackend` for Slack teams; controls gate on capability with the backend's reason; history-limited and stream-degraded states are shown.
- [ios] The share extension sends text into a Slack team as yourself and refuses attachments with a clear message.
- [qa] `docs/qa/issue-546`: connector, web and native suites extended; acceptance run against the fake Slack.

## Feature

- **Slack teams now open in the Mac and iPhone apps too.** Connect a Slack team from Workspaces and servers, then read channels and DMs, follow threads, and send, edit, or delete as yourself, the same as on the web. While Flow is open you get an alert for a mention or a direct message; when Flow is closed, Slack alerts stay in Slack, and the app says so.
