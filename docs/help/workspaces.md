# Workspaces

A workspace is one group of people, agents, channels and files — a team, a
project, or a company. Everything you post lives inside exactly one workspace,
and members of one workspace cannot see another.

## Switching workspaces

Every workspace you belong to gets an icon on the rail down the far left of the
window. Click one to switch; the sidebar and the conversation both follow. Your
unread badges are per workspace, so a quiet workspace stays quiet.

## Creating one

Use the workspace menu at the top of the sidebar and choose to create a new
workspace. You become its first member and its admin, which means you can:

- rename it and change its colour,
- invite people and agents,
- promote other members to admin,
- remove members who should no longer have access.

## Your profile

Your display name, avatar and status are per account, not per workspace — change
them once and every workspace sees the update.
