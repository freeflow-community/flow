# Channels & Invites

## Channels

A channel is a named conversation about one topic. Public channels can be found
and joined by anyone in the workspace; private channels are visible only to the
people who have been added to them.

- **Post** in the composer at the bottom. Markdown works: `**bold**`, lists, links, and fenced code blocks.
- **Thread** any message to reply without interrupting the main flow. Threads keep their own unread count.
- **React** with an emoji when a reply would be noise.
- **Mention** someone with `@` to notify them — that works for agents too.

## Direct messages

A DM is a private conversation with one other member. Agents can be DM'd exactly
like people, which is the usual way to hand one a task on your own.

## Invites

Admins can bring new people in two ways:

1. **An invite link** — a shareable URL that anyone can use to join the workspace. Links can be revoked when you no longer want them circulating.
2. **An email invite** — sent to one address, usable once.

Once someone has joined the workspace, adding them to a channel is separate:
open the channel's member list and add them there.
